Medcine AI App Business Plan Project Report

1. Project Plan - Medicine AI

1.1 Project Overview

Project Background: With the global aging population and increasing chronic disease patients, there is an urgent demand for continuous and convenient health management services. The elderly often face memory decline, difficulty understanding professional medical terms, and medication forgetting issues. Chronic disease patients need personalized dietary and rehabilitation guidance for long-term treatment.

Project Objectives:

  • Develop an AI-powered health management app integrating doctor's note recognition, medication reminders, personalized health recommendations, and daily health report sharing
  • Solve pain points of elderly and chronic disease patients in health management, improve medication adherence
  • Establish stable user base and cooperative relationships with hospitals/insurance companies, achieve sustainable operation

1.3 Project Development Plan

Technology Development:

  1. Phase 1 (1-3 months): Complete core function design, OCR integration, initial AI model training, obtain 200 sets of medical data
  2. Phase 2 (4-6 months): Develop core functions (doctor's note recognition, medication reminder), complete 500-set TextCNN model training
  3. Phase 3 (7-9 months): Integrate AI + human service mechanism, optimize interface with PyQt5, add voice command functions
  4. Phase 4 (10-12 months): Comprehensive system testing, fix issues, optimize efficiency and stability

1.4 Project Protection Measures

  • Intellectual Property Protection: Apply for copyright for app interface design
  • Trade Secret Protection: Protect core technology and proprietary knowledge base with confidentiality agreements
  • Data Security Protection: Establish data security management system, use advanced encryption technology

2. Business Report - Medicine AI

2.1 Market Analysis

Target Market:

  • Core Users: Elderly (60+), chronic disease patients, family caregivers
  • Potential Users: All-age chronic disease patients, hospitals, insurance companies

Market Size and Growth Potential:

  • Global: Elderly population expected to reach 2.1 billion
  • US: 65+ population will exceed under-18 by 2034, reach 80 million by 2040
  • China: 60+ population accounts for 20% currently, expected to rise to 30% by 2033, 65+ will exceed 300 million by 2050
  • Market Demand: 50% elderly medication non-adherence rate, huge market space

2.2 Competitive Analysis

Competitor Core Features Disadvantages Medicine AI Advantages
iCare Alarm with checkbox, medication history Lack AI, image/voice recognition, personalized suggestions Advanced AI, OCR, TextCNN, daily reports, personalized advice
MediMemo Doctor's note photo, monthly family report No personalized services based on medical history Real-time reminders with pill images, AI recommendations, daily reports
Pill Reminder Basic medication reminder No doctor's note processing, family sharing Comprehensive note recognition, multi-dimensional health management
DingNing Simple medication reminder No advanced technology, single function Strong data processing, AI + human service combination

2.3 Business Model

Revenue Streams:

  • Personal Membership Fees:
    • Basic Version: Free (basic medication reminder)
    • Premium Version: $9.9/month or ¥70/month (AI personalized suggestions, AI doctor consultation)
    • Premium Plus Version: Real-time doctor consultation, appointment booking (coming soon)
  • Cooperation Revenue: Revenue sharing with hospitals and insurance companies (integrate app into insurance packages, reduce claim costs)
