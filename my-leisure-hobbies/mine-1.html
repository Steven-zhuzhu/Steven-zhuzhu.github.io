EOL (End of Light) Mob Farm is a cutting-edge technical mob farm exclusive to Minecraft Java Edition, renowned as one of the most efficient mob farms in the game’s community.

The widely cited "800 million items per hour" is a theoretical/exaggerated value used for marketing purposes. In practical survival gameplay, the actual output is much more realistic and achievable for regular players.

"Efficiency is not about numbers on paper, but about practicality in survival gameplay." – Minecraft Technical Community
  • Basic EOL Farm: 3-6 million items per hour
  • Optimized with Looting III + TNT: 8-9.5 million items per hour (nearing 10 million/hour)

EOL Light Suppression Mechanism
(Minecraft Technical Demo)

① Light Suppression

Uses redstone/update suppression mechanics to force the brightness of Nether Portal blocks to 0, permanently meeting the "dark environment" requirement for mob spawning—this eliminates any light-based restrictions on mob generation.

② Portal Cutting

Removes obsidian from the portal frame while leveraging update suppression/skip logic to keep the portal structure intact. This creates an infinite mob-spawning area with no spatial limitations.

These two core mechanisms are the foundation of EOL Mob Farm's extreme efficiency. Unlike traditional mob farms that are limited by light levels and spawn areas, EOL breaks through these constraints completely.

AFK 64 Blocks

What I Do as a Player

  • Precisely build the light suppression core and Nether Portal spawn platform (critical for maintaining 0-light conditions)
  • AFK within 64 blocks of the farm to keep chunks loaded and ensure continuous mob spawning
  • Optimize TNT placement (3×3 block spacing) + wield a Looting III sword enchanted with Smite V/Bane of Arthropods V to trigger player kill mechanics
  • Collect and auto-sort all drops (gunpowder, bones, rotten flesh, arrows, enchanted gear) using hopper and chest systems
  • Store loot in large-scale shulker box storage systems for long-term resource management
  • Utilize collected loot for bone meal production, explosive crafting, villager trading, and endgame gear enhancement
