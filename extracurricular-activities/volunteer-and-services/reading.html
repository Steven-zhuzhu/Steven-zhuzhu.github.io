📚Reading Connects the World

Due to delayed language development in childhood, I came to know the world through reading rather than words. When God closes a door, He opens a window—and for me, this window is reading. It unlocks the unknown world, lets me see the broader picture through words, and makes reading a lifelong passion.

🎙️ What a wonderful journey it’s been! --From Childhood Audio Books

I observed the world with my eyes and understood it through books. Once I could speak clearly, I was eager to share what I'd read. I launched a reading column on China's Ximalaya Audio, reading aloud for others—my classmates were my first small audience. Listening back now, my voice was childish and my pronunciation unsteady, as I still struggled with dysarthria. These audio recordings remain there quietly, available to listen to anytime. Here is the link to my Ximalaya channel:https://www.ximalaya.com/zhubo/46168181

🔬 To My Physics Little Book Club

In junior high, I started a book club for English mystery and sci-fi novels. In senior high, I founded a quantum physics book club—anyone interested joined the discussions, and my dorm was our debate battlefield.
